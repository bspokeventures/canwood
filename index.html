Approach

How a project runs.

A renovation puts a crew in your house for months. Here’s how we run one.

  1. 1

    A walk through your house

    We meet at your house, look at what’s there and talk through what you want. You’ll get an honest read on what’s realistic before anyone draws anything.

  2. 2

    A scope and a real number

    A written scope, priced line by line, before any work starts. If something changes along the way, you see the new number before we act on it, not after.

  3. 3

    Drawings and permits

    Every city on the west end has its own permit rules, and Westmount’s are famously thorough. We handle the applications and coordinate with your architect or designer. If you don’t have one, we’ll introduce you to one we trust.

  4. 4

    The build

    One site lead, and you’ll have their number. Floors covered and dust walls up before demolition starts. The site swept at the end of every day. A short update every week so you know where things stand.

  5. 5

    Handover

    We walk through every room with you, write down anything that isn’t right, and fix it. And we still pick up the phone after the final invoice.

A note from Zachary

The houses we work in were built by people who took their time, and I think the work we add should hold up the same way. That means finding out what’s behind a wall before we open it, choosing materials that will still look right in twenty years, and leaving your house clean at the end of every day, because you’re still living in it while we’re there.

If that’s the way you’d like your house treated, give me a call.

Areas

Four neighbourhoods we know well.

Each has its own kind of house. Driving times are from Westmount.

  1. Office

    Westmount

    Turn-of-the-century stone and brick houses, most with their original details still in place. Our office is here.

  2. About 10 min

    Hampstead

    Tudor and Georgian revival houses on wide lots. Many are ready for a new kitchen, a new bathroom, or both.

  3. About 15 min

    Côte Saint-Luc

    Post-war bungalows and split-levels with good bones and small rooms. We open them up.

  4. About 30 min

    West Island

    Beaconsfield, Pointe-Claire, Baie-D’Urfé and Dorval. Mid-century houses near the lake, and the bigger projects that come with more room.

Somewhere else on the island? Ask us. The right project is worth the drive.
